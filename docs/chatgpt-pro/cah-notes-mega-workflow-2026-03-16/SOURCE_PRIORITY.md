Use the uploaded sources in this order of priority:

1. **Primary factual source**
   - `Louisa Leone MD3 2025 - Child and Adolescent Health - Study Notes.pdf`

2. **Secondary factual source**
   - `CAH Bible (shared 2025).pdf`

3. **Tertiary concept-hint source**

How to apply this:

- Facts, management points, diagnostic distinctions, investigation interpretation, and counselling points should come from the Louisa notes whenever possible.
- Use the CAH Bible mainly to fill gaps or confirm details when the Louisa notes are brief.
- The question zip should mainly help identify what is likely exam-relevant or historically emphasised.
- Do **not** copy or paraphrase stems from the question zip.
- If a topic appears in the question zip but is not clearly supported in the note PDFs, either skip it or keep the claim very conservative.
- Prefer citing the Louisa notes over the CAH Bible, and prefer both note PDFs over the question zip.

What to avoid:

- treating the past-question zip as the main writing template

- one-step question-doc association items that recreate prior exam stems
