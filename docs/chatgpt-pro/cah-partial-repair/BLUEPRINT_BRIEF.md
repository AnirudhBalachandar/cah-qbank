# CAH KAT Blueprint Brief

Exam structure:

- Total questions: 60
- Format: multiple-choice questions with clinically related scenarios
- Skills assessed across the paper:
  - history taking
  - physical examination
  - investigation interpretation and ordering
  - diagnosis
  - clinical reasoning
  - management
  - population health

Curriculum area weighting:

- General Paediatrics: 16 questions, 26.7%
- Paediatric Sub-specialties: 12 questions, 20%
- Paediatric Surgery: 10 questions, 16.7%
- Emergency Paediatrics: 10 questions, 16.7%
- Adolescent Medicine: 6 questions, 10%
- Community-based Paediatrics: 6 questions, 10%

Suggested target distribution for a 24-question draft batch:

- General Paediatrics: 6
- Paediatric Sub-specialties: 5
- Paediatric Surgery: 4
- Emergency Paediatrics: 4
- Adolescent Medicine: 2
- Community-based Paediatrics: 3

Suggested fallback distribution for a 12-question draft batch:

- General Paediatrics: 3
- Paediatric Sub-specialties: 2
- Paediatric Surgery: 2
- Emergency Paediatrics: 2
- Adolescent Medicine: 1
- Community-based Paediatrics: 2
