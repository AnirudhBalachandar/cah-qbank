# Rejected Patterns Summary

The previous two ChatGPT Pro batches were rejected by the local originality screen because they remained too close to existing imported CAH stems.

## What failed

These direct task patterns are too close to the source corpus and should be avoided:

- stable gastroenteritis child tolerating oral fluids -> discharge on oral rehydration
- no early haemoglobin or reticulocyte response to oral iron -> non-adherence
- low total IgA plus negative TTG IgA plus villous atrophy -> coeliac disease
- low CSF glucose plus raised protein and pleocytosis -> bacterial meningitis
- hypernatraemic dehydration -> water deficit exceeds sodium deficit
- anaemia compensation -> increased 2,3-DPG
- pneumatocele after pneumonia -> Staphylococcus aureus
- focal seizure onset -> brain lesion
- loud pansystolic murmur at left sternal edge -> small VSD
- recurrent febrile UTI -> DMSA for renal scarring
- symptomatic swallowed magnet in bowel -> emergent laparotomy
- tender groin lump in infant -> analgesia and transfer to paediatric surgery
- delayed meconium passage at 48 hours -> Hirschsprung disease
- bilious vomiting plus double bubble -> duodenal atresia
- hot coffee burn -> cool running water for 20 minutes
- expiratory hyperinflation after failed salbutamol -> inhaled foreign body
- trauma series -> lateral cervical spine plus AP chest plus AP pelvis
- deteriorating trauma child with unequal pupils and shock -> urgent intubation
- transition planning least important factor -> specialist rather than GP transfer
- parental alcohol supply -> alcohol dependence
- four prior tetanus doses plus rusty nail -> no tetanus-specific treatment
- posterior rib fracture -> non-accidental injury
- oral white spots then descending rash -> measles

## What to do instead

Use the same internal material, but move to adjacent exam tasks such as:

- next best counselling point
- best follow-up step after the obvious immediate action
- which additional finding would support or weaken the diagnosis
- which investigation answers a different but related clinical question
- which child is higher risk and why
- most important prevention or safety-netting advice
- best discriminator between two plausible diagnoses
- which result would change management
- which history feature should be specifically sought next
- which longer-term complication or consequence matters most

## Strong constraints

- Do not lightly paraphrase the rejected stems.
- Do not preserve the same hallmark clue bundle plus the same lead-in.
- Do not use a classic one-step association unless you substantially change the question task and the main reasoning step.
- If a concept is too thin to support a truly new question, skip it.
